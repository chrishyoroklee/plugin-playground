Starting Python

Use it in Ableton Live

  1. Press Download and take the Max for Live device.
  2. Drag the file onto a track in Live (Suite, or Standard with Max for Live): an audio track for an effect, a MIDI track for an instrument.
  3. Every dial here is a Live parameter: automate it, map it, save it with your set.

For other hosts, the same device compiles to a VST3, CLAP or Audio Unit plugin: see Download.

What you hear is checked against Max itself: the filter, distortion, crush, delay and reverb match a Max 9 render sample for sample. Chorus, flanger and tremolo differ only in where their sweep starts.

Open source, built on MaxPyLang. The recordings are CC0 from Freesound (credits); the other sounds are computed in your browser.
