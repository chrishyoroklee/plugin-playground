# Sample credits

The clips on the interactive plugin page are public domain (Creative Commons 0), taken
from Freesound. Each was level-matched (peaks at -6 dBFS) and re-encoded to 128 kbps
MP3. The three electric guitar clips were also cut to a loop, from Freesound's
high-quality previews: `electric.mp3` is the whole recording, `riff.mp3` is 13.6 s
from 0.24 s in, `strat.mp3` the first 16 beats; nothing else was changed. CC0 asks for no credit, and the people who recorded them get one anyway.
